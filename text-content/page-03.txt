Tamanna Moolchandani
Founder & CEO | 971 Hospitality
At the heart of 971 Hospitality is Tamanna Moolchandani, Founder & whose vision is
centered on creating meaningful hospitality experiences where exceptional food,

and attention to detail.Her leadership philosophy combines creativity with execution,

together, and creates lasting impressions through food and service.
