# venue-events — (no separable assets)

The 971 Hospitality source material (the 15-page company profile deck, the layered
PSDs, and the standalone files) does **not** contain any standalone venue or event
photography that can be cleanly separated as an individual asset.

The deck's imagery is food photography, founder/leadership portraits, brand
medallion logos, and the global-footprint map. Event/venue context only appears
baked into full-page slide compositions — those are preserved in `../../pages-reference/`.

This folder is kept for structure and future venue/event photography.
